All Roads Lead to Rome

Flow-driven Multi-Anchor Exploration
for Open-Environment Active 3D Mapping

Anonymous Authors
NeurIPS 2026 Submission
▤Paper ◇Method ▶Videos

Preserving multiple exploration hypotheses for efficient active 3D mapping in unseen environments.

Overview

  • We study active 3D mapping in open environments, where partial observations and scene shifts make a single long-horizon goal difficult to predict reliably.
  • We formulate exploration as conditional multimodal anchor generation. Conditional Flow Matching preserves multiple plausible goals, while obstacle-aware planning converts them into executable paths.
  • Exploration-mode clustering reduces redundant candidates. Hierarchical selection chooses an exploration mode and reranks its paths to produce the next trajectory.
